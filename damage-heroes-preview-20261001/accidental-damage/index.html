UNDERSTANDING ACCIDENTAL DAMAGE INSURANCE CLAIMS

Accidents happen, at home and in businesses, but accidental damages can be costly. While accidental coverage can cover you for smaller claims like wine spills and toddlers ruining furniture- bigger accidents can happen too. Trees fall on guests' vehicles, other vehicles may crash into your home or building and more.

If you have damages that were caused by an accident, your repairs or replacements may be covered by accidental damage coverage in your insurance policy. But, as with many other claims, insurance companies work to pay as little as possible for accidental damage claims. If you have damages, it’s time to call a public adjuster that you can trust to work for your claim. At United Claims Specialists, we are here to help you get the most for your accidental damage claims.

How are accidental damage claims settled?

There are many different types of accidental damage claims, and no matter the claim, you need a skilled public adjuster to make the most of your claim. At United Claims Specialists, our skilled public adjusters understand how to help clients get the highest possible payments for their accidental damage claims. We work with many types of accidental damage claims, such as fallen objects. Each policy has specific provisions for accidental damage claims.

THESE MAY NOT BE COVERED BY ACCIDENTAL DAMAGE INSURANCE:

 

Pet damages
Wear and tear
Gradual deterioration
Deliberate damages
Defective workmanship
 

The above may not fall under the umbrella of accidental damage, but they may be covered by other components of your residential or commercial policy.
