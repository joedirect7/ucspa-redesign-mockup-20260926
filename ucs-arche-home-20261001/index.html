Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Short answers for the moments after a loss — before you sign anything or accept an early number as final.

Free Claim Consult
What does a public adjuster do?
A licensed public adjuster represents the policyholder — not the insurance company. UCS documents the loss, prepares and presents the claim, and negotiates with the carrier on your behalf.
When should I call UCS?
As early as you can after a covered loss — ideally before you accept an initial payment as final, and before incomplete scope or cause statements get locked into the file.
How does UCS get paid?
United Claims Specialists gets paid when you get paid — contingency aligned with recovery, with fees disclosed under applicable state rules. Fee percentages vary by state. No outcome is guaranteed.
Is the first offer usually final?
Often, a first offer is a starting scope after a short inspection — not a complete repair budget. Missed damage and depreciation gaps are common. A second look against a supported estimate can matter.
What is the first step?
A free claim consult. Tell us what happened; we’ll tell you whether a public adjuster fits your situation. Call 855-321-5677 or start online.
