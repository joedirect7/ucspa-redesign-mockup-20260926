WHAT ARE CLAIM SUPPLEMENTS?

Claim supplements are when you submit an estimate to the insurance company that contains additional items that were not included in the insurance adjuster’s initial estimate.

WHY YOU SHOULD SUBMIT ROOF INSURANCE CLAIM SUPPLEMENTS:

When a claim is first brought to the attention of your property insurance company, the first thing they do is inspect the damage and provide an initial damage estimate for your claim. This estimate is exactly that, an estimate.

It is also up to the contractor performing the repairs to assess the property for damages as well.

This is where claim supplementing comes in to play. The contractor must create their own estimate and add any additional items that they feel are necessary to complete the repairs properly and submit this estimate to the insurance for approval. The contractor must also submit with their estimate evidence supporting the need for each additional line item they are requesting.

This process typically happens before the repairs are completed, but occasionally when repairs get underway the contractor will find additional damage that was not considered during the initial estimate process. These additional line items can add up over the course of repairs and are only discovered once the repairs are underway.

United Claims Specialists submits your claim supplements to the insurance company to ensure you are compensated for the money you deserve.
