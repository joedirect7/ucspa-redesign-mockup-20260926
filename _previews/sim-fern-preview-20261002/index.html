Why hire a public adjuster

Their adjuster works for them

An insurance adjuster works for the insurer. Their estimate can come in lower than what contractors quote for repairs, which can leave you paying out of pocket.

You need a full accounting of damage

Insurers may miss items or limit coverage. A public adjuster documents the loss carefully so you pursue what your policy actually covers.

Roof damage has clear thresholds

In Florida, when a large share of the roof is damaged, a full replacement may be required. Insurers sometimes argue the damage is just under that line — we help verify the facts.

Denials and low offers happen

Claims can be denied or limited over installation, design, or wear issues. Experienced public adjusters know how to present the claim so it stands on solid documentation.
