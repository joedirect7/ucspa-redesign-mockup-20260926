New Jersey Claim Help FAQ

Licensed public adjusters where your NJ property sits. Soft-power answers — free consult anytime.

Is UCS Licensed for New Jersey Property Claims?
Hire (and verify) a licensed public adjuster where the damaged property sits. UCS serves New Jersey with local licensed public adjusters. Soft path: Insurance Claim Help or call (855) 321-5677. See also Locations.
What Does a Public Adjuster Do?
A licensed public adjuster represents you — the policyholder — on a property insurance claim: inspecting and documenting damage, preparing a supported estimate package, explaining coverage in plain language, and communicating with the carrier so you are less likely to misspeak on the record. Soft next step: Insurance Claim Help.
How Does UCS Get Paid?
We get paid when you get paid — contingency aligned with recovery, with fees disclosed under applicable state rules. Fee percentages vary by state. No outcome is guaranteed. Ask during a free consult what applies where your property sits.
Should I Accept the Insurance Company’s First Offer?
Often a first offer is a starting scope after a short inspection — not a complete repair budget. Pausing for a second look before you treat the number as final can matter. A licensed public adjuster can compare a first offer to a supported estimate. Soft path: Insurance Claim Help.
