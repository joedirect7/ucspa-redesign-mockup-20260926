We help owners, property managers, communities, and contractors file property damage claims, pursue a fair settlement and eliminate the headaches of dealing with insurance claims. Don't file another claim without us!

Residential Claims

Our teams of public adjusters have successfully settled tens of thousands of residential claims. We speak the insurance "language", understand the ins and outs of insurance policies, and know how to navigate the oftentimes, grueling claims process. Our experts are trained to not only assist in the claims process, but to look for, and document damages that may not be obvious; ultimately pursuing the full amount the policy covers.

Commercial Claims

Our team is made up of trained professionals who take a proactive approach to each client's claim, crafting a customized solution to pursue a fair settlement and taking swift action on behalf of our clients. We then show the insurance company what and why they owe and make sure everything your policy actually covers is obtained based on the damages at hand.

We negotiate all types of property Damage Claims against ALL Insurance Companies… ** SUCCESSFULLY! **
