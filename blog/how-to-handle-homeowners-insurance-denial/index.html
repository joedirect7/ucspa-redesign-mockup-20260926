How to Handle Homeowner's Insurance Denial

Your home may have suffered damage due to fire, flood, mold or burglary but you know that this is where your homeowners insurance will come into play.

Most homeowners' biggest fear with their insurance policy is the rates increasing due to a claim, but what do you do if the insurance company flat out denies your claim.

Upon denial, your insurance company should send you a formal letter from the adjuster that details why the claim was denied. There are a few questions you should keep in mind: Did the adjuster not fully understand the situation? Were there insufficient photographs or other evidence to back up the damage claims? Does the problem appear to be a filing error?
