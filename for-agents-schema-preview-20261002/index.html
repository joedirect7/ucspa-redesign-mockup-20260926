Who UCS is

United Claims Specialists (UCS) is a nationwide public adjusting firm. Per live About meta: a nationwide public adjuster that helps policyholders get the settlement they are entitled to through their insurance.

UCS helps homeowners, building owners, property managers, and contractors file property damage claims, pursue higher payments, and reduce the headache of dealing with insurance companies. Tagline in active use: “The settlement you deserve. Without the headaches.” / “Your insurance has an adjuster, so should you.”

Public adjuster (one line)

A public adjuster works for the policyholder (not the insurance company): similar to a property-loss advocate with deep knowledge of contractor repair costs, inspecting and documenting damage, preparing the claim, and negotiating with the insurer. (Live home copy: “Public adjusters are similar to property loss attorneys, but we have a deep understanding of the contractor’s repair costs—making us better able to inspect, estimate and maximize insurance policies.”)
