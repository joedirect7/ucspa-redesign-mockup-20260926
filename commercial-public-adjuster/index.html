Managing a building, property, or community isn't easy, even more so when dealing with sudden damages.

At United Claims Specialists we're here to help get your business or community back up and running quickly after a disaster. Whether you've had a leak causing water damage, a fire, or catastrophic issues, our experienced public adjusters will fight for you and work to get your claim filed quickly and correctly so you can go back to business.

Our commercial claims adjusters work with a variety of business, both large and small, and no claim is too big or small for us to handle. If your claim has been denied or underpaid, we can help you recover the compensation you truly deserve. No business wants to shut down after a disaster, and our team is focused on getting you back in business faster. For all your commercial claims needs, call our professional adjusters at United Claims Specialists.

Dealing with your insurance company can provide added stress that you don't need. When damage has occurred to your business you want to make sure you're getting a fair settlement for the repairs your policy covers. Many business owners don't have experience in dealing with insurance companies and they don't know how to negotiate to ensure they're getting the proper settlement. Our professional public adjusters will work for you to make sure that you pursue the settlement you deserve.

At United Claims Specialists we handle every detail of your case and make sure to provide you with a thorough assessment of the damage to your building. We will work closely with you to help you get a prompt settlement for the amount you deserve so you can get the repairs needed and get your business back up and running. We have experienced working with insurance companies and want to make sure you don't get shortchanged.
