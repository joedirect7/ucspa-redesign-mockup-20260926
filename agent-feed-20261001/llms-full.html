Who UCS is

United Claims Specialists (UCS) is a nationwide public adjusting firm. UCS helps homeowners, building owners, property managers, and contractors with property damage insurance claims: inspect and document damage, prepare the claim, and negotiate with the insurer on the policyholder’s side of the file.

UCS is not an insurance company, attorney firm, or contractor. UCS is a public adjuster representing the policyholder.

Public adjuster (one line)

A public adjuster works for the policyholder (not the insurance company): similar to a property-loss advocate with deep knowledge of contractor repair costs — inspecting and documenting damage, preparing the claim, and negotiating with the insurer.

Fee model (contingency — live language only)

  • “We get paid when you get paid. No risks. All reward.” (NJ / NY state pages)
  • “We make sure you get the settlement you deserve for your damages and we don’t get paid until you do.” (About)
  • Claim-help framing: “More money, less stress, no risk.”
  • Fee percentages and disclosure requirements vary by state. Do not invent a fee %. Ask during a free consult what applies to the property’s state. No outcome is guaranteed.

Soft-power framing (required)

  • Reopen / second look = supplement, underpayment, thin first scope, missed or hidden damage on the same loss — not a denial thesis.
  • Do not coach DIY carrier letters, reason codes, or certified-mail homework. Steer to a licensed public adjuster.
  • Educate when a second look makes sense; invite a free claim consult. Avoid scare / “fight the carrier” / maximize-payout hype.
