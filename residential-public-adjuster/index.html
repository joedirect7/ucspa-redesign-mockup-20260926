First Steps

The American Dream has always been to own a home. You've worked hard, saved and made one of the most significant purchases in your life. You've paid your insurance premiums for years and now damage has occurred. Most people's first step is to call their insurance provider to file a claim but STOP and think about it before you make the call.

What To Consider

  • Is it in the insurance company's best interest to settle your claim for the highest amount possible?
  • Do you know exactly what your policy covers?
  • Do you have years of experience negotiating with the insurance company?
  • Is your insurance company's in-house adjuster looking out for your best interest?
